# Origins in Leonardo’s Library

This skill draws structural primitives and methods from the books Leonardo listed or sought:

- **Geometry & Mechanics**: Euclid, Archimedes (On the Centre of Gravity, De Ponderibus), Vitruvius, Alberti, treatises on proportions and algebra.
- **Natural Philosophy**: Aristotle (De Caelo, De Mundo, works on motion and the Nile), Pliny, Theophrastus, Albertus Magnus.
- **Hydraulics & Engineering**: Frontinus (Aqueducts), De Re Militari, practical notes on water, canals, and machines.
- **Anatomy & Medicine**: Avicenna, Galen, Alessandro Benedetto, works on utilities and health.
- **Architecture & Structures**: Vitruvius, Alberti, notes on castles, palaces, arches, and foundations.

The continuous treatment of ratio, flow, force, vessel, channel, and equilibrium across these texts is the source of the cross-domain method.
